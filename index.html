Common questions

How much does office space cost vary by area in London?

Across the 181 private-office listings behind this map, price per desk per month (normalised for office size) ranges from about £141 to £3,200 — a wide spread depending purely on postcode, with a citywide median near £550/desk/month. Mayfair and Covent Garden sit at the top (roughly £860–£980/desk/month), while Whitechapel and Hackney & Bethnal Green sit closer to £200–£300/desk/month for comparable desk sizes.

What's the cheapest area for office space in London?

Among areas with more than a handful of listings, Hackney & Bethnal Green (~£302/desk/month) and Stratford & Bow (~£388/desk/month) are the most affordable core-London submarkets we track. Outer submarkets can price lower still, but with far fewer comparable listings.

What's the best area for a small team's first London office?

For a first office, most small teams prioritise low cost and good transport over a landmark postcode — which points to Zone 2 areas like Hackney & Bethnal Green, Stratford & Bow or Whitechapel, all under £400/desk/month with direct links into the centre. See "Which London area fits your team?" above for matches by industry.

Is coworking cheaper than a private office?

Generally yes — coworking works out cheaper than a private office in the same area for a small team, since you're not paying for unused space. Across the areas we track, coworking prices average roughly £200–£750/desk/month against £200–£980/desk/month for a private office; the private office earns its premium once a team needs its own walls, lockable storage or room to grow into. How current is this data?

This snapshot is compiled from public flexible-workspace listings and operator data across London. It's a point-in-time reference, refreshed periodically — see the methodology note below for how prices are normalised.

How prices are calculated

Private-office prices are normalised to £ per desk per month by dividing each listing's advertised "from" price by the smallest office size it applies to — this avoids a 30-desk suite skewing an area's number just because it's a big space. Coworking figures are the advertised monthly rate for an ongoing desk, not a day pass, so both metrics are directly comparable across areas. Figures are aggregated from public marketplace and operator listings, not myHQ's own booking data, and reflect a single dated snapshot rather than live availability.

Broadly, price tracks Tube zone more than any other single factor: Zone 1 core areas (Mayfair, Soho, the City) sit at £620–£980/desk/month, Zone 1 fringe (Shoreditch, Farringdon & Clerkenwell, Borough & London Bridge, Holborn) at £525–£640, and Zone 2–3 areas (Hackney, Stratford, Canary Wharf) drop to £300–£520 — often for a shorter commute than a "cheap" Zone 1 postcode once you account for space per person.

Areas with 2 or fewer listings are marked "small sample" in the ledger — treat those figures as directional, not definitive. "Best for," area descriptions and the operators listed in the compare view are representative characterisations for this prototype, not a verified or exhaustive directory.
